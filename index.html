Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 2026

Welcome to Royal Chess Clock. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting any information associated with your use of our chess clock and timer application. This Privacy Policy details how data is handled, stored, and utilized, including our integration of third-party advertising partners like Google AdMob.

1. Zero Data Collection (100% Local & Offline-First)

We do NOT collect, transmit, sell, or store any personal data on external servers. Royal Chess Clock operates as a private, offline-first application. We do not require accounts, logins, email addresses, phone numbers, contacts, or location access.

The following information is created during your use and is stored strictly on your local device (via AsyncStorage) and never leaves your phone:

  • Player Profiles & Names: Your chosen display names, avatar icons, ELO ratings, and preferred board side.
  • Match History & Analytics: Timestamps, move counts, winner records, clock presets, and move think times.
  • Preferences & Audio Settings: Sound profile selection, tactile haptic switches, 10s warnings, and millisecond display options.

2. Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) & Third Parties

To keep Royal Chess Clock free and accessible to chess players worldwide, we integrate Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) to serve advertisements (such as banner ads).

Google AdMob and its advertising partners may collect and process certain device identifiers and telemetry:

  • Advertising ID: Google Advertising ID (GAID on Android) or IDFA (on iOS) used for ad attribution and frequency capping.
  • Device & Network Information: Device model, operating system version, screen size, IP address, and general network connection state.
  • Ad Performance Metrics: Impressions, ad clicks, views, and interaction telemetry to prevent invalid ad traffic and fraud.
Learn how Google uses data from apps ›

3. Data Retention & Complete Data Deletion

Because 100% of game data is stored exclusively on your device, we retain zero copies of your personal data on any server.

  • Instant Data Deletion: You can permanently erase all player profiles, match history, and preferences at any time by going to your Android device settings (Settings › Apps › Royal Chess Clock › Storage & Cache › Clear Storage/Data), or by simply uninstalling the application.
  • Ad Personalization Controls: You can opt out of personalized ads or reset your Google Advertising ID on your Android device at any time (Settings › Google › Ads › Reset or Delete Advertising ID).

4. Children's Privacy (COPPA)

Our application is designed for general audiences and chess enthusiasts of all ages. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13. If you believe that personal information has been inadvertently collected, please contact us immediately.

5. Security

We value your trust. All match statistics and custom configurations are stored locally on your device. No sensitive personal information is stored or transmitted.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically to reflect new app features, regulatory standards, or ad network updates. Any revisions will be reflected on this page with an updated effective date.
